# Performance recording and historical artwork credits

The Performance Room contains twenty-two real performer and ensemble recordings, not synthesized or AI-generated performances: thirteen unaccompanied instrumental solos, two chamber works with piano (a clarinet–bassoon–piano trio and a bassoon sonata duo), one wind quintet, four soloist-with-orchestra recordings (a bassoon concerto, two Debussy harp dances with string orchestra, and Mozart's concerto for flute and harp), and two purely instrumental orchestral recordings. Accompaniment is allowed under the listening policy; choral works are excluded, and no accompanied work is labeled as a solo. Fourteen source files were obtained from Wikimedia Commons, including the Marine Chamber Orchestra's two Debussy dances and Michael Laucke's Bach guitar performance; seven performances came from IMSLP, the Petrucci Music Library; and the Open Goldberg Variations recording came from its official open project via the documented GitHub mirror below. Local playback copies were converted to MP3 at 160 kbps for iPad/browser compatibility. The items explicitly marked as excerpts contain only the first 160 seconds; the other recordings contain the complete source performance. No endorsement by any performer or ensemble is implied.

Audio refresh, 3 November 2026: all twenty-two local playback recordings were processed again with adaptive FFT noise reduction before their final 160 kbps MP3 encoding. Non-guitar recordings received conservative 4–5 dB reduction with noise tracking and gain smoothing. Three parameter strengths were compared for each classical-guitar recording; the four guitar files without an earlier denoising pass use a moderate 6–7 dB setting, while Michael Laucke's already-denoised Bach recording received only a second, deliberately light 3 dB pass. This refresh used the current local playback copies because the external original-source download channel was unavailable; it did not retrieve or substitute different performances. No additional trimming, pitch/tempo processing, or loudness normalization was applied. All twenty-two output files passed complete decoding and duration checks. The per-item changes below retain the earlier editing and licensing history and are supplemented by this refresh note.
